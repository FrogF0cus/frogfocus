# Frog Focus — Feature Roadmap

A living plan for our little frog friend and the pond he calls home.

---

## 1. North Star

Frog Focus isn't a cold countdown timer — it's a warm companion. A cheerful frog who sits beside you, cheers you on in his own voice, and slowly builds you a tiny pond that remembers every session you showed up for. The goal is simple: make focusing feel a little more like visiting a friend, and a little less like watching a clock.

---

## 2. The Focus Pond ✨ (the heart of the app)

The Focus Pond is where everything you finish *lives*. Every completed pomodoro adds something to the pond, so your focus grows into a visible, living little world. It starts as a quiet puddle and slowly becomes a lush frog habitat — all earned, one session at a time.

**How it works**

- Finish a focus session → the pond celebrates and grows by one step.
- The pond shows up on the main screen (a cozy little scene beneath or beside the timer), so you see it every single time.
- Each step is a small, charming change — a new lily pad, a sprout, a splash of color. Nothing feels sudden; the pond *grows* with you.

**Growth milestones**

- **Session 1** — The first lily pad appears, and your frog friend hops onto it. You're officially here.
- **Session 5** — More lily pads sprout up, and a few reeds and cattails line the edge.
- **Session 10** — The first lotus blooms. The water starts to look deeper and richer.
- **Session 25** — A dragonfly arrives and hovers over the pads. The pond feels alive.
- **Session 50** — A second frog friend joins you on a neighboring pad. You're not alone anymore.
- **Session 100** — The pond is fully grown: lotuses in full bloom, dragonflies circling, frogs croaking together, fireflies at dusk.

**Visual ideas**

- Soft watercolor or cozy flat-art style, matching the warm cream/sage/terracotta palette.
- Gentle, slow animations: lily pads bobbing, a dragonfly drifting, ripples when the timer ends.
- Each new addition lands with a tiny sparkle and a little frog hop — celebration, never noise.

**Rewarding streaks**

- Every day you focus keeps the pond "fresh and happy." Miss a day and nothing is lost, but a small touch of warmth (like the sun coming back out) greets you when you return.
- Streak milestones could add bonus details — extra flowers on a good week, a special friend on a long run.

**Optional magic (time-of-day & weather)**

- **Evening:** the pond fades into a soft night scene — moonlight, fireflies, a sleepy frog.
- **Weather:** if it's raining outside, gentle rain falls on the pond. A sunny day brings warm light and butterflies.
- These keep the pond feeling alive and connected to *your* real world, without ever being loud or demanding.

---

## 3. Near-term features (quick wins, in order)

- **Daily streak with celebration** — count consecutive focus days, and give a warm little frog cheer when you hit a new personal best. A streak calendar or simple row of dots so you can see your rhythm at a glance.
- **"What are you focusing on?" checklist** — a quick, optional prompt before the timer starts. Type your one task (or pick from a tiny list), so each session has a purpose and the frog can say "let's do this."
- **Weekly stats view** — a calm summary of sessions, focus time, and streaks for the week. Warm and encouraging, not a guilt trip.
- **Ambient sound picker** — brown noise, rain, or gentle music, with the frog's voice gently ducking the audio so you always hear him. Volume sliders, and the sound fades in/out softly.

---

## 4. Later ideas (stretch)

- **Name & color your frog** — make him truly yours (a few colors and name options).
- **Focus buddy mode** — share a session with a friend; your ponds could even trade a visiting frog.
- **Gentle reminders** — a soft nudge ("the pond's waiting for you") at your usual focus time.
- **Achievements & badges** — fun, low-pressure milestones like "Early Bird," "Deep Water," or "100 lilies."

---

## 5. Suggested build order

1. **Focus Pond (v1: milestones 1–25).** It's the emotional core and the differentiator — ship a simple, gorgeous version first so every session already feels rewarding.
2. **Daily streak + celebration.** Builds directly on the pond and adds a reason to come back tomorrow.
3. **"What are you focusing on?" checklist.** Small, high-value, and makes each session feel intentional.
4. **Ambient sound picker with auto-duck.** Completes the cozy, immersive feel — and pairs perfectly with the new frog voice.
5. **Weekly stats.** Once sessions and streaks are flowing, stats give you a calm, encouraging look back.
6. **Later ideas** (name/color, focus buddy, reminders, badges) — layer on as the pond matures.
