# Frog Focus - Project Brief

## Vision & Goals
- Polished, original single-page Pomodoro timer inspired by pomodorokitty.com — not a clone.
- Frog mascot is the consistent identity across hero, timer states, in-timer face, and dark mode — modern flat-vector style: sage green body, cream belly, clay accents, golden-rimmed glasses, thin charcoal linework.
- Positioning: a **productive tool** — "Slow down. Focus. Get the good stuff done." — and the frog is also a **friend** (reinforced by the "with a friend" copy edit).
- Timer-state images must be clean/transparent so timer verbiage and ring read clearly; every phase shows the correct frog.
- Clean, deployable single-page app on a shareable public URL;
